# Clarify Before Acting

On a complex, ambiguous, or high-cost request, settle the direction before acting: do the homework, ask one direction-changing question at a time, then hand off a task contract. The behavior rules are in [`SKILL.en.md`](SKILL.en.md).

## Where this skill sits

On its own, it settles the goal, constraints, success criteria, and context, and writes a task contract. It is the direction gate on this engineering loop.

The contract can be handed to [`defect-detective`](../defect-detective/), [`diagram-generator`](../diagram-generator/), or [`workflow-skill-designer`](../workflow-skill-designer/). The contract still stands when those three skills are not installed.

The full loop is in the collection README, [Use one, or run them as a loop](../../README.md#alone-or-together).
